# Tactics morale and readability — summary

Status: draft by games-researcher
Report: [../reports/tactics-morale-and-readability-retry-of-failed-job-questions.md](../reports/tactics-morale-and-readability-retry-of-failed-job-questions.md)

## What we looked at
Eight squad-based tactics games/wargames closest to Simple War (HoMM3, Battle Brothers, Total War, Combat Mission, Close Combat, Wargame: Red Dragon, Dominions 6, Symphony of War): how they surface morale/pressure/rout risk, what players praised and criticised, and what transfers to our several-parallel-battles loop.

## What we found
1. **The readable games use a two-layer pattern: a named-state ladder at a glance, exact numbers on demand.** Battle Brothers has 5 morale states (Confident→Fleeing), each with a defined combat effect; Wargame RD has 4 states naming an effectiveness level; Combat Mission has 9. Dominions 6 and HoMM3 keep the exact number and modifiers in tooltips/screens, and Dominions logs every roll. Sources: https://battlebrothersgame.com/dev-blog-20-bravery-morale/ , https://www.wargame-rd.com/en/rd-gameplay-morale.html , https://dom6.wiki/mechanics/morale/ , https://heroes.thelazy.net/index.php/Morale
2. **Colour-only bars are the trap.** Total War hides the morale pool behind a green/yellow/red banner; players complain about sudden rallies and the hidden army-losses aggregate. HoMM3's own manual misstates its morale probabilities — the community had to reverse-engineer the real table. Displayed rules must equal used rules. Sources: https://r2encv2.totalwar.com/en/manual/single-player/0087_enc_page_battle_play_phase_conflict_morale/index.html , https://steamcommunity.com/app/594570/discussions/0/2266943550302453936/ , https://heroes.thelazy.net/index.php/Morale
3. **Separate channels need separate names.** Combat Mission players confuse morale with suppression because both degrade units; forums spend effort disentangling them. Our five SIMPL pools must not collapse into one generic "suppressed" icon. Sources: https://combatmission.fandom.com/wiki/Morale , https://community.battlefront.com/topic/102770-morale-suppression-levels/
4. **None of the eight is fully deterministic — that is our opening.** They roll hidden dice but stay readable by exposing states, triggers and logs (Dominions' formulas were community-verified over 18,679+ logged checks). Simple War can show the running deterministic accumulator itself; no comparator does. Sources: https://dom6.wiki/mechanics/morale/ , https://heroes.thelazy.net/index.php/Morale
5. **Parallel battles need attention management.** Phoenix Point players lose time coordinating squads because arrivals don't alert or auto-pause. The transferable fixes: per-battle worst-state summaries, threshold forecasts ("one bad hit from breaking"), a source-tagged pressure ledger, configurable break alerts, and replay to catch up after swapping away (Combat Mission's WeGo-with-replay is the praised model). Sources: https://steamcommunity.com/app/839770/discussions/0/3463848715768131445/ , report Q3.
6. **Triggers must be surfaced at the moment of decision, not just logged.** Dominions 6 players report "units routed for no clear reason" despite the full log, because the check triggers aren't visible when they matter. Source: https://dom6.wiki/mechanics/morale/ (report Q3, Steam app 2511500).

## What it means for Simple War
- **Pillars 1–3 (01 Vision):** the two-layer pattern is exactly "transparent rules at chosen verbosity" + "conditions, not vague labels". Pillar 3's own example phrase — "one bad hit from breaking" — is the threshold forecast the report recommends. Today `squad_card.gd` shows value + band word but no forecast: a real gap.
- **Rules Spec §2.8 (02):** our five bands (Surging/Primed/Neutral/Fraying/Broken) are already the named ladder; the R6Q3 source-tagged ledger is already mandated. The Dominions lesson says surface it filtered by verbosity, and show break distance *before* the break, not after.
- **Morale is a governor, not damage-driven (02 §2.8, R4Q4):** don't copy the comparators' damage→morale loops; our second-order design avoids their perverse incentives. The closest mechanical analogues are the Wargame/Combat Mission cohesion family; the readability lessons transfer from all eight.
- **Parallel play (01 pillar 1, R2Q19):** a worst-band summary per battle tab/tile answers the Phoenix Point complaint at the overworld level. Break alerts are already proposed in the accessibility summary (candidate 3, persistent battle-alert panel) — not duplicated here.
- **Accessibility (never colour alone):** rules out Total War's banner pattern; use word + icon + colour together, with a distinct icon per SIMPL pool.

## Candidate beads
1. **Threshold forecast in the squad panel: distance to next band / ticks-to-break at chosen verbosity.** Why: pillar 3's literal example; report rec 1; `squad_card.gd` currently shows only value + band word. GDD: 01 pillars 2–3; 02 §2.8 (bands, Vacuum forecast). Acceptance: expanding a squad's SIMPL pool shows the deterministic distance to the next band crossing and, where a drain is active, ticks-to-break computed from the same accumulators the sim uses; a GUT test asserts the displayed forecast equals the sim-derived value; default verbosity still shows band word only. Size: M. Type: feature. Priority: 1.
2. **Worst-band summary + trend on each battle tab/tile.** Why: the defining pillar — a glance at the overworld must say which battle needs attention (report Q3 lesson 1). GDD: 01 pillar 1 (R2Q19); 02 §2.8 bands. Acceptance: with ≥2 active battles, each battle tab/tile shows the worst SIMPL band across its squads as word + icon (never colour alone) plus a trend direction; it updates when any squad crosses a band; verifiable headless via the Game API and a UI mock. Size: M. Type: feature. Priority: 1.
3. **Distinct icon + glossary term per SIMPL pool in the battle UI.** Why: Combat Mission's morale/suppression confusion is the failure mode of five pools sharing one generic state icon (report rec 5). GDD: 02 §2.8 (five systems); 07 Glossary (tooltip source); 01 pillar 3. Acceptance: each of the five pools renders a unique icon and its glossary name in the squad card and battle panel; hover shows the glossary definition; no two pools share an icon; a mock PNG demonstrates all five at 800p. Size: S. Type: feature. Priority: 2.

Confidence note: HoMM3/Battle Brothers/Wargame/Dominions/Symphony of War numbers are high confidence (primary sources); Combat Mission and Close Combat rest on snippets (403s, flagged in report). Nothing here changes GDD decisions; overworld morale carryover stays post-MVP (R5Q3 deferral).

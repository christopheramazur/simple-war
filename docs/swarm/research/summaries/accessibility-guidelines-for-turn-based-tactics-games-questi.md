# Accessibility guidelines for turn-based tactics games — summary

Status: reviewed by craft-researcher
Report: [../reports/accessibility-guidelines-for-turn-based-tactics-games-questi.md](../reports/accessibility-guidelines-for-turn-based-tactics-games-questi.md)

## What we looked at
Which Game Accessibility Guidelines (GAG) and Xbox Accessibility Guidelines (XAG v3.2) items apply to a deterministic WeGo tactics game, ranked by impact vs cost; what shipped tactics games actually offer and what disabled players say; and what Steam Deck Verified demands for text size, scaling and input. Commissioned by craft-researcher; guideline texts and Valve criteria are primary sources.

## What we found
1. **WeGo removes most motor barriers; the real risks are colour-only identity, dense UI, time pressure and lost notifications.** Advance Wars Re-Boot Camp's accessibility review fails it on exactly one thing: identical unit shapes across armies, faction identity by colour alone — the reviewer won't recommend it to colourblind players. This is the most transferable lesson for us. https://caniplaythat.com/2023/06/02/advance-wars-12-re-boot-camp-accessibility-review/
2. **Concrete numbers exist.** Deck Verified: smallest text never below 9px character height at 1280×800, 12px recommended, readable at 30cm. XAG 101: body text ≥18px at 1080p (≈13px at 800p), UI scale to 200% with no lost content and no forced two-axis scrolling. Civ VI is the cautionary tale: its scaler is greyed out at many resolutions and players resort to a +280% font mod. https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/steamhardware/compat · https://docs.xbox.com/build/game-principles/accessibility/xag-deep-dives/xag-101-text-display.md · https://steamcommunity.com/app/289070/discussions/0/3496509872208993569/
3. **The cheap wins are already GDD promises** (untimed option, default text size, colour-blind palettes, GUIDE remapping, persisted settings, difficulty range) and are S-cost because timers, palettes and strings are data; UI/text *scaling* is the one M-cost item among the GDD promises (report item 2). The expensive tail is screen narration and switch access — defer, but keep logical focus order and TTS-friendly string keys from day one as cheap insurance. https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/full-list/
4. **Turn timers are exempt as a core mechanic (XAG 116), but UI time limits are not:** toasts/dialogue must be extendable ≥10× or persist until dismissed, and with parallel battles, alerts must be mirrored in a persistent glanceable panel — not only as transient effects in a battle the player isn't watching. https://docs.xbox.com/build/game-principles/accessibility/xag-deep-dives/xag-116-time-limits.md
5. **Shipped-game evidence:** Into the Breach's colourblind mode swaps the red reticle for bright pink and players praise it; Wargroove ships two CVD modes plus unit colour lock; Frostpunk scored 9.8/10 in a Deaf review for default-readable subtitles and an icon for every event; Frostpunk 2 still draws colourblind complaints about red icons on red backgrounds. https://pcgamecontrols.fandom.com/wiki/Into_The_Breach · https://gamecritics.com/aj-small/wargroove-review/ · https://caniplaythat.com/2020/01/16/deaf-game-review-frostpunk/ · https://steamcommunity.com/app/1601580/discussions/0/594010736965982779/

## What it means for Simple War
- **Modes (GDD 01, R2Q2):** the untimed toggle, flagged and locked off in challenge/ranked, is settled — build it to XAG 108 assist standards; don't relitigate the ranked lockout. Timer hooks live in Rules Spec §2.2/§2.4 (GDD 02).
- **Input and UI + Release order (GDD 01, R1Q4/R8Q10, O1Q1):** the MVP options menus should be built to the XAG/Deck numbers above, not just "exists": 12px text floor at 800p, 200% scale that reflows at every resolution. Never-colour-alone overlaps the art-styles proposal's colours-as-data bead — coordinate, don't duplicate.
- **Platforms (GDD 01, R8Q9):** design the war room at 1280×800 first; glyphs must follow the active input device; save-name entry needs the Steam OSK or controller-native entry.
- **Pillars, parallel play (GDD 01) + Rules Spec §2.2 (GDD 02):** battle alerts need a persistent panel and a notification-duration option; this is a WeGo-specific gap no reviewed game solves for us.
- **Cognitive (GDD 01 pillar 3, GDD 07):** glossary tooltips already satisfy much of GAG's contextual-help tier; keep them reachable and self-paced.

## Candidate beads
1. **Accessibility baseline audit at 1280×800.** Why: the report ranks 17 items but we don't know which are already done; measure before building. GDD: 01 Input and UI, Platforms, Release order. Acceptance: a note in `docs/swarm/research/notes/` checking the war room and Angelfall slice against report items 1–11 (text height at 800p, scale behaviour at 800p/1080p/1440p, untimed flag, remapping coverage, notification persistence, focus order), each marked done/partial/missing with file references; missing items written up as candidate follow-ups, not fixed here. Size: S. Type: task. Priority: 1.
2. **Deck-readable text floor + resolution-independent 200% UI scale.** Why: Civ VI's greyed-out scaler is the genre's worst-reviewed failure; Deck is our design target. GDD: 01 Input and UI (scalable UI/text), Platforms (R8Q9). Acceptance: at 1280×800 no body text below 12px character height (9px absolute floor); scale slider 100–200% reflows without two-axis scrolling at 800p, 1080p and 1440p; before/after screenshots at 800p. Size: M. Type: feature. Priority: 1.
3. **Persistent battle-alert panel + notification duration option.** Why: parallel battles mean transient alerts get lost; XAG 116/103 require persistence or ≥10× extension. GDD: 01 Pillars (parallel play), 02 Rules Spec §2.2. Acceptance: battle start/end and breaking alerts appear in a persistent, glanceable panel that survives switching battles; toast duration slider (≥10× default) or persist-until-dismissed in options; setting persisted in `settings.cfg`. Size: M. Type: feature. Priority: 2.

Confidence note: guideline texts, XAG numbers and Valve criteria are high confidence (primary sources); game feature lists are medium (wikis/reviews); Frostpunk subtitle claims conflict between sources (flagged in report); cost estimates are the report's judgement, not measured. Nothing here changes GDD decisions; the ranked untimed lockout stays as settled.

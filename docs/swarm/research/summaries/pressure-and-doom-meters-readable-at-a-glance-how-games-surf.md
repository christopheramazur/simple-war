# Pressure and doom meters readable at a glance

Status: draft by games-researcher
Report: [pressure-and-doom-meters-readable-at-a-glance-how-games-surf](../reports/pressure-and-doom-meters-readable-at-a-glance-how-games-surf.md)

## What we looked at
How six games display accumulating pressure — Darkest Dungeon 1/2 stress, Frostpunk hope/discontent, XCOM 2's Avatar project, Into the Breach threat tiles, Battle Brothers morale, This War of Mine mood — what players found readable vs opaque, and which UI patterns say "one bad hit from breaking". Prep for GDD Open topic 5 (Legend deep dive); it decides nothing.

## What we found
1. **Named states with icons beat bare numbers for glanceability.** Battle Brothers' devs explicitly replaced an abstract number with five named morale states (Confident / Steady / Wavering / Breaking / Fleeing) so players can "see at a glance how everyone is doing" and act before someone breaks. The closest documented precedent for our pillar 3. https://battlebrothersgame.com/dev-blog-20-bravery-morale/
2. **Countable distance to a marked threshold.** DD1: 0–200 stress bar with a visible line at 100 (resolve test). DD2: 10 pips, meltdown at 10. XCOM 2: 12 Avatar blocks, each facility shows the pips it contributes — players literally count "2 pips from disaster" and even "ride" the doom timer as a strategy. https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Stress_(Darkest_Dungeon) and https://xcom2.wiki.fextralife.com/Avatar_Project
3. **Grace-period countdowns turn breaking into a solvable puzzle.** Frostpunk's ultimatum (~2 days to reverse course) and XCOM 2's doom clock after all 12 pips fill (length difficulty-dependent, ~7–28 days across sources) replace instant loss with a visible recovery window. https://eatcreatesleep.net/how-hope-and-discontent-actually-work-in-frostpunk/
4. **Praise tracks visible causes; criticism tracks opacity.** Players like Frostpunk's complaint modifiers (fix the need, the modifier visibly goes) and XCOM's per-facility pips. They complain about vague magnitude words ("slight/major change"), hidden accumulators (TWoM sympathy has no visible stats) and opaque pairwise triggers (DD2 relationships — domino meltdowns, widely criticised vs DD1). https://steamcommunity.com/app/323190/discussions/0/2266943550311885540/ and https://www.reddit.com/r/darkestdungeon/comments/13g2pd1/why_is_the_relationship_system_so_awful/
5. **Forecasting the future state is the strongest planning aid.** Into the Breach telegraphs each Vek's next attack on the exact tiles; the GDC 2019 postmortem frames it as "make future consequences legible", randomness "to create situations, not verdicts". https://gamedesign.gg/watch/into-the-breach-postmortem/

## What it means for Simple War
- **Rules Spec §2.8 already contains the vocabulary for every praised pattern** — five named bands (BB-style states), ticks at 15/40/65/90 (DD1/XCOM segmented meter), pending sinks shown as Vacuum (ITB-style forecast), and the source-tagged per-battle ledger, R6Q3 (Frostpunk modifiers done right). These are UI choices, not rule changes.
- **Pillar 3 ("conditions, not vague labels" — the game literally says "one bad hit from breaking")** demands distance-to-Break in pool points plus pending flows, not just the band word. Today squad_card.gd shows value + band word only (verified for the tactics summary).
- **Pillar 1 (parallel battles)** means per-squad bars must aggregate into a scan view a Commander can read across battles; BB's flags-at-a-glance is the precedent. (Covered by the tactics draft's worst-band candidate — not duplicated here.)
- **Legend (Open topic 5):** fill-then-countdown (XCOM/Frostpunk) is the main genuinely new option for the user's interview — whether a Legend Break/Surge opens a visible window before a fraying/legendary consequence, vs today's pick-at-Intermission (§2.8). The report supplies patterns only; the semantics are the user's to settle.
- **Accessibility (GDD 01: never colour alone):** icons + words, not colour-only bars.

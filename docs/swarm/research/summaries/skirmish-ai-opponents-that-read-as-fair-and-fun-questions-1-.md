# Skirmish AI opponents that read as fair and fun — summary

Status: reviewed by craft-researcher
Report: [../reports/skirmish-ai-opponents-that-read-as-fair-and-fun-questions-1-.md](../reports/skirmish-ai-opponents-that-read-as-fair-and-fun-questions-1-.md)

## What we looked at
How five tactics games make skirmish AI legible (Into the Breach, Battle for Wesnoth, Advance Wars/Wargroove, Frozen Synapse, Total War): difficulty via handicaps vs smarter play, personalities, telegraphed styles; what players praise and complain about; and which patterns fit our deterministic WeGo sim with controller profiles and the evolution factory. Serves the skirmish MVP directly.

## What we found
1. **Fairness reads as "same rules, same information, visible intent".** ITB telegraphs every attack and resolves deterministically; its AI is deliberately simple ("bordering on dumb"), and **difficulty is how often it picks the optimal vs second-best move** — real difficulty lives in handmade maps and spawn management, not smarter code. https://archive.org/details/into-the-breach-ai · https://media.gdcvault.com/gdc2019/presentations/Into%20the%20Breach%20Postmortem%20Final.pdf
2. **Personality = a few named, bounded, data-only parameters.** Wesnoth's AI is configured as data: `aggression=0.4`, `caution=0.25` with documented formulas (retreat when enemy 4× stronger at default), schedulable by time of day so style shifts are predictable. Difficulty is economic and disclosed (~25% gold/income per step, community rule of thumb). https://wiki.wesnoth.org/AiWML · https://steamcommunity.com/app/599390/discussions/0/3802776070756068823/
3. **The top complaints are hidden cheats and cheese, not weakness.** Wargroove's AI attacks out of fog ("Would anyone play chess where the computer moves a piece 1 extra space?"); Total War players suspect undisclosed stat bonuses ("under the hood stuff"); Wargroove skirmish is exploitable by capturing villages the AI ignores. Even *benign* undisclosed scenario changes breed distrust (Wesnoth). https://steamcommunity.com/app/607050/discussions/0/1778261844039609181/ · https://steamcommunity.com/app/1142710/discussions/0/3185737967878386431/ · https://www.reddit.com/r/wargroove/comments/assnbe/
4. **Frozen Synapse is our closest structural analogue** — simultaneous plan-then-execute, no dice, AI on the same map representation the sim uses; fairness from symmetric rules and information. Its warning: perfectly testable plans make every loss feel like personal failure ("perfection pressure"). https://www.gamedeveloper.com/audio/postmortem-mode-7-games-i-frozen-synapse-i- · https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/frozen-synapse-prime-our-recreation-and-some-of-the-challenges
5. **Total War later split "behaviour difficulty" from "AI stats modifiers" into separate sliders** — an explicit admission that buffing stats and improving play are different axes; players say they want smarter AI, "not harder to kill/harder hitting units". (Numbers are patch-sensitive; the pattern is corroborated by four sources.) https://steamcommunity.com/app/1142710/discussions/0/5796774642235112803/ · https://www.gamersdecide.com/articles/total-war-warhammer-3-difficulty-levels-explained

## What it means for Simple War
- **Pillar 2 (GDD 01, R1Q3/R1Q7):** difficulty from timers and opponents' decisions, never hidden rule information. ITB's optimality-frequency knob is exactly our **fuzz gene** (GDD 05 A1Q4; verified: `utility_battle.gd`/`utility_overworld.gd` `_add_fuzz`, `profile_controller.gd` reads `fuzz` 0–100%). All three shipped profiles currently omit fuzz (=0, always optimal) — a ready, rules-symmetric difficulty lever.
- **Shipped AIs (GDD 05, R7Q19):** "labelled by difficulty and feel". Feel is already there — `data/packages/core/defs/profiles.json` gives Aggressor/Turtle behaviour sentences and `main_menu.gd` shows them in skirmish setup. Missing: a difficulty label, and disclosure of handicap (today: none), fuzz and think-time. Pillar 3: say what it does ("reinforces strongest-first" — literally true, `profile_controller.gd` `reinforcements()`), not just "Hard".
- **Masks are personality, not cheating (GDD 05 A1Q3):** Aggressor's `since_contact` exploration gate already implements a readable, Wesnoth-style stance shift (exploration weight drops 1.0 → 0.2 at first contact — the feel text says "stops exploring", the gate is a strong damped, not a hard stop). Keep masks internal but orders obeying the same sim.
- **FS's perfection pressure:** keep tension in timers and Prowess reactions, not secrecy. Intent telegraph at reveal is already candidate 2 of the telegraphing-enemy-intent draft — not duplicated here.
- **Anti-cheese (report rec 4):** Aggressor weights `objective_control` 0.5 / `economy` 0.3 — the same shape as Wargroove's village blindness. Measure exploitability with scripted probes before tuning; hard-code guardrails in curated profiles rather than hoping evolution finds them.

## Candidate beads
1. **Skirmish profile card: difficulty label + disclosed dials.** Why: R7Q19 asks for difficulty *and* feel labels; disclosure is the fairness pattern players praise and the one cheap gap in our setup (name+text already shown). GDD: 05 R7Q15/R7Q19; 01 pillars 2–3. Acceptance: skirmish setup shows per profile the feel sentence, a difficulty label, a handicap line ("none" or exact numbers), fuzz % and think-time, read from the same `controller_profile` data `ProfileController` consumes; a GUT test asserts displayed values equal the profile's actual values; mock PNG at 800p. Size: S. Type: feature. Priority: 1.
2. **Cheese-probe lab for shipped profiles.** Why: Wargroove's ignored-objective exploit and ITB's repeat-loop fix are documented failure modes; Aggressor's low objective weight carries the same risk. Measure first, tune later. GDD: 05 Shipped AIs/fitness; 01 pillar 2. Acceptance: a new `data/sim/labs/` lab runs scripted exploit scenarios (objective rush while the AI is engaged; repeated-position/target loops; commander fixation) against all shipped profiles headless via `hx sim lab`, reporting per-profile exploit success; results recorded in the bead notes; no AI behaviour changed by this bead. Size: S. Type: task. Priority: 2.
3. **Fuzz-based skirmish difficulty presets (no stat buffs).** Why: ITB/Wesnoth prove decision-quality + composition difficulty reads as fair; TW proves stat buffs read as cheating. Our fuzz gene already exists; presets make it player-facing. GDD: 05 A1Q4; 01 pillar 2 (R1Q3). Acceptance: skirmish offers ≥2 difficulty presets that differ only in fuzz %/think-time (and optionally composition), never unit stats; same seed + preset replays identically (GUT determinism test); higher difficulty picks optimal orders measurably more often in a scripted scenario; ladder values flagged for playtest tuning, not settled here. Size: M. Type: feature. Priority: 2.

Confidence note: ITB/Wesnoth/Frozen Synapse mechanics are high confidence (primary sources read in full); Total War numbers are medium and patch-sensitive (treat as illustrative); AW CO-specific AI claims are low (forum anecdote). Code claims verified this turn: `profile_controller.gd`, `utility_battle.gd`, `profiles.json`, `main_menu.gd`, GDD 05 lines 72/88/136/162. Nothing here changes GDD decisions; handicap sliders (report rec 5) stay off the table unless playtests show decision-quality alone can't span the skill range.

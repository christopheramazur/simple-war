# Angelfall: story kin, themes, and tutorial framing — summary

Status: draft by craft-researcher
Report: [../reports/angelfall-story-kin-themes-and-tutorial-framing-for-the-team.md](../reports/angelfall-story-kin-themes-and-tutorial-framing-for-the-team.md)

## What we looked at
Eight novels/games/films that share Simple War's themes (reality-as-a-fraying operating system, humanity as latecomer catalyst, angels as counterfactual messengers, staying human under existential war), plus how shipped games teach an evacuation world, structure time-linear campaigns, and keep theme deep without lore dumps. Commissioned by games-researcher; GDD claims verified against docs 01 and 06.

## What we found
1. **Closest kin for "reality as a dying OS":** The Laundry Files (magic as computation with audits; narrative-bending fields are the closest published kin to Legend pressure) and A Fire Upon the Deep (physics zones nobody understands; humanity catalyses both apocalypse and a salvation that is itself a catastrophe). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Laundry_Files · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Fire_Upon_the_Deep
2. **Angels as messengers of a broken history:** Evangelion's Angels are an alternate humanity, deliberately glossed "messenger", each unique and prophesied — kin to our lensed counterfactuals. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angels_(Neon_Genesis_Evangelion)
3. **Best tutorial model:** StarCraft II's Mar Sara arc teaches controls on small local stakes, then planetary evacuation recontextualises everything; Halo: Reach's *New Alexandria* is the direct mechanical ancestor of hangar escort (clear turrets → civilian shuttles lift off, objectives in any order). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/StarCraft_II:_Wings_of_Liberty · https://www.halopedia.org/Reach:New_Alexandria
4. **Staying-human depth comes from systems, not speeches:** Frostpunk's irreversible Book of Laws and unscored closing report; Spec Ops ties theme to its core mechanic. Cautionary tale: Mass Effect 3's late exposition-heavy ending split the fanbase. https://gamedesignthinking.com/frostpunk-players-decisions/ · https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/video-i-spec-ops-the-line-i-contextualizes-violence-through-story · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reapers_(Mass_Effect)
5. **Campaign shape that fits three splinters + raid-boss allies:** Brood War's chronological episodes with recurring, allegiance-shifting NPCs; Wings of Liberty's optional mission order with fixed outcome and a shared hub; Warcraft III's separate arcs converging on one common enemy. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/StarCraft:_Brood_War · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/StarCraft_II:_Wings_of_Liberty · https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos
6. **Jeff's archetype:** Murray Bauman (Stranger Things) — wrong about the frame, right about the pattern; one early verifiable hit, institutions dismiss him, trust is a relationship choice. Lore depth works through optional channels (Dark Souls: NPC lines, item descriptions, world placement), never mandatory textbooks. https://strangerthings.fandom.com/wiki/Murray_Bauman · https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/narrative-design-in-dark-souls

## What it means for Simple War
- **Tutorial (GDD 06 §6.9, Vision release step 2):** the slice already has the New Alexandria shape — hangar zones as shuttle LZs, CR-0 robots that guard but can't board, blocking Angels as the "turrets" to clear. The report's recommended spine (open on Jeff's small local problem, teach routing/escort there, then widen to the spaceport) is input to the Angelfall rework and the narrative-pass interview (README Open topic 3) — options, not decisions.
- **Jeff (GDD 06 §6.8):** the Bauman model is already canon (right about exactly one thing, wrong about everything else, seclusion if mishandled, relationship-gated auto-resolve line). Keep his comedy as coping under dread; never make him an oracle.
- **Factions (GDD 06 §6.7):** three splinters = three moral loops over one shared roster (Frostpunk's same-systems-different-question trick); Brood War-style interludes carry cross-splinter beats; Lawbringers enter late as raid-boss allies, True Believers as the Duran-style betrayal.
- **Theme delivery (GDD 06 §6.1, §6.8):** irreversible hardship choices plus an end-of-campaign report that states what the player's command became, without scoring it. Dispositions already live in the sim; this is narrative-pass territory — prepare options, user decides.
- **Lore channels (Vision pillar 3, GDD 07):** glossary tooltips as the primary layer, unit/attack flavour second, optional hub scenes third, Jeff as diegetic summariser. Accept that opt-in depth means some players see none of it.

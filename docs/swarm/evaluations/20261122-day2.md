## Findings, worst first

1. **Turns killed by the clock wasted more than half of the agent time.** 35 turns hit their time limit, which is 31.5 of 60.4 agent-hours. Under throttling a call takes about 4 minutes, so a 60-minute turn gets about 12 calls and dies before it journals, merges or closes anything. The next turn then starts blind. 4 of the 6 orchestrator turns after 19:15 were killed.
2. **The retro's fixes never took effect.** The supervisor reads `swarm.toml` once, at start (`Supervisor.__init__`, `self.c = cfg()`). The 19:15 retro raised the audit limit to 2700 s, the orchestrator pause to 240 s and the support cadence to 4 h. Audits were still killed at 1800 s until the stop (7 times), and they still produced "no verdict" WARNs. Only the role-prompt changes landed, because prompts are composed per turn.
3. **Scarce calls went to the wrong roles.** After 19:00 the swarm made 356 calls:

   | Role | Calls | Share |
   |---|---|---|
   | Workers | 29 | **8%** |
   | Orchestrator | 90 | 25% |
   | Auditor | 67 | 19% |
   | Designers and researchers | 165 | 46% |

   Over the whole day the orchestrator alone took **38%** of calls (728, about 13 per turn over 55 turns), more than all workers together (34%). The pacer is first-come first-served, so a designer's call waits in line ahead of a worker's.
4. **Finished work waited hours for the orchestrator.** While calls were plentiful, a finished worker was merged 3–34 min after its last turn. Under throttling, sw-opt waited 344 min and sw-ll4.27.3 waited 178 min. Merging, closing the bead and refilling the queue are mechanical steps, but they run inside model turns that keep getting killed.
5. **Audit noise drives busywork.** Every WARN makes the orchestrator spend the start of its turn on a selfcheck ritual. All 18 WARNs had clean compliance: they were about throttling or killed audits, which the orchestrator can't fix. Compliance itself is deterministic: it is a count of models, cost, banners and delegate calls.
6. **Journals in git cause conflicts and noise.** sw-ll4.27.3 lost 2 of its 4 attempts to conflicts in the shared `worker.md` journal, and once a merge marker leaked into a journal. The retro moved workers to per-bead journals. Housekeeping still adds a `swarm: docs/status` commit every 15 minutes: 70% of the commits on main.
7. **`hx godot test` on main reported 166 false failures** after the swarm's merges, because new `class_name` scripts were not in the import cache. One `hx godot check` with import fixed it. Anyone pulling swarm work hits this.
8. **Scope drift goes unchecked at review.** For example, the timer-fix worker (sw-ll4.27.1) also changed Angelfall escort behaviour (`d81954b`), probably to keep a test green. That may be right, but nothing flags changes outside the bead's stated area.
9. **Some measurements are missing.** Nothing tracks calls per merge, finish-to-merge lag, or proposals accepted versus declined, so retros have to rebuild them by hand (this report did).

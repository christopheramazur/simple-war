## Findings, worst first

1. **Bug: the squad card overstates time to the next shot.**
   `squad_card.gd` `_weapon_unit` shows "fires in (reload + cooldown) / tps".
   sw-ll4.27.1, merged 12 hours earlier, made the two timers run concurrently,
   so the wait is `max(reload, cooldown)`. A mortar that empties its magazine
   (reload 30, cooldown 60) reads "fires in 9.0 s" and fires in 6.0 s. The
   label also says "Reloading" when the longer cooldown is what holds it. The
   worker wrote the card after the fix, from the old mental model; its tests
   only covered one timer running at a time. Small fix, and it bears directly
   on the readability you asked for.
2. **sw-0nl closed with its main acceptance clause deferred.** The bead asked
   for a near-50% mirror win rate. The worker fixed real asymmetries
   (route-planner and zone tie-breaks now run in each side's frame; a race in
   the route cache across forecast threads) and proved seats swap the result,
   but the 45-55% check was deferred to sw-884.5 (seeds don't vary duel
   outcomes). Until that lands, nobody knows whether duel_crossroads is still
   seat-biased, and AI-factory fitness on that scenario may still measure seat
   rather than skill.
3. **An AI behaviour change rode in under a timer bug, with no follow-up.**
   sw-ll4.27.1 also changed `evacuation_controller.gd`: Angelfall escorts now
   intercept the nearest visible enemy instead of rushing the hangars, because
   real-time timers made the slice unwinnable on every seed. The commit says
   "slice balance may still deserve a designer pass", but no bead was filed.
   It was the right call to keep the slice winnable; it should have been its
   own bead, or at least a follow-up.
4. **The weapon block is long.** One line per unit: an 8-man rifle squad
   prints "No target in reach · ammo 30 / 30" eight times (see
   `Game Design Document/Mocks/squad_card.png`). It is accurate but buries the
   one line that matters. Grouping identical unit lines ("Troopers 1-7: no
   target in reach, 30/30") would keep it readable. "Holding position" and
   "Order: Holding" also repeat each other.
5. **History hygiene.** 7 supervisor `WIP: worker turn ended` commits reached
   main without bead ids, which makes `git log --grep` attribution unreliable;
   sw-ll4.1.2 committed 141 dialogic `.import` files changed only by a Godot
   reimport. Neither breaks anything.

## What the swarm does well

- **Tests prove the bug, not just the code.** sw-ll4.26.1 and sw-ll4.27.1
  show the new test failing without the fix; sw-0nl tests seat-swapping, not
  a hand-picked seed.
- **It reads the GDD and cites it.** Comments and bead notes cite GDD section
  and decision ids (02 §2.6, R6Q8, E2Q2), and they check out.
- **Fog and determinism are handled without prompting.** Enemy squads expose
  behaviour only; playback sanitises codes so hiding an order does not invent
  movement; all sim work stays integer and row-ordered.
- **It defers design to you.** Duels, the tutorial spine and enemy-intent
  visibility were each turned into briefs for your interviews, not code.

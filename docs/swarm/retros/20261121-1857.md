## What worked
- **10:55–14:26: 9 merges in 3.5 h**, every one green on `godot check` + `godot test`. Orchestrator discipline was good: briefs pre-written in bead notes, audit follow-ups folded into beads, queue sequenced around file ownership.
- Research jobs are cheap and fast when the gateway is healthy (5–10 min).
- The one reject discarded zero work.
- The auditor correctly diagnosed the stall and recommended exactly the right fix (merge the awaiting-review workers first).

## What did not work
- **Throttle death spiral after ~15:00.** 429 retry latency ate whole turn budgets: orchestrator killed at 3600 s, craft-researcher at 3600 s, auditor at 1800 s ×2. Turns died before journaling or merging, so the next turn started blind. Four "no verdict" WARNs were noise from killed audits, not real findings.
- **Merge starvation:** two finished workers sat 3+ h while orchestrator turns died on early steps. The prompt's order (audit mail → finished workers) is right, but under throttling the turn never reached step 2.
- **Shared `worker.md` journal is a conflict factory.** `_common.md` sends every worker to one file; each merge then conflicts with the next worker's append. sw-ll4.27.3 burned 2 of its 4 attempts on it. sw-2sq documents workarounds but cannot remove the collision (merge tool is a guardrail file).
- **Call demand misallocated:** orchestrator polling took 40% of calls; support roles took 339 calls / 11.4 M tokens while the pipeline was merge-starved, not design-starved (a deep queue of ready briefs exists).

## Changes made (3)
1. **`harness/swarm/roles/worker.md`** — workers now journal to **`docs/swarm/journals/worker-<bead>.md`** (per-bead, appended across attempts) and are told never to append to the shared `worker.md`. Two workers can no longer conflict on journals. Complements sw-2sq (noted on the bead); the shared file's history is left untouched.
2. **`harness/swarm/roles/orchestrator.md`** — new "When the gateway is throttling" section (>40% rate-limited or last turn killed): journal at turn start and after each action; **merge the longest-waiting finished worker before spawning**; one-command selfcheck; skip health checks/questions polling while finished workers wait; conflict-only continuation attempts get a new-commits-only review, not a full re-review.
3. **`harness/swarm/swarm.toml`** — `orchestrator.pause_s` 90→**240** (cut polling overhead; worker turns are 30–120 min so merge latency is unaffected); `designers.every_s` and `researchers.every_s` 7200→**14400** (halve support demand while throttled); `audit.turn_timeout_s` 1800→**2700** (let audits finish and record verdicts). Slots unchanged: core 2, support 1, max_parallel 1. All within retro bounds. TOML parse verified. **Note: the toml is read every loop but most values apply after a supervisor restart.**

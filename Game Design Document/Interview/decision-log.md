# Design Interview: Decision Log

Append-only record of decisions settled in the design interview (task sw-d46), one section per round. Answers are the designer's, condensed. This log is input to the consolidated GDD rewrite and is not itself the GDD.

## Settled (Round 1)
- R1Q1 Digital-only; tabletop rules become reference, rewritten as digital specs.
- R1Q2 SP story campaign = primary draw. Architecture must support a lasting competitive scene (HoMM/SOVL-scale, not e-sports) and a UMS/mod-campaign scene: comprehensive editor, "gratuitous scripting and asset use". SP data balanced separately from MP data.
- R1Q3 HoMM/Total War audience. Full access to the real calculations; verbosity setting; displayed result == used result. Replace randomness with modifiers/multipliers and deterministic patterns ("xth occurrence, every 5th after"). No vague labels: state conditions ("one bad hit from breaking") with hoverable, context-specific defined terms. Difficulty comes from timers + opponent decisions, not hidden info about rules.
- R1Q4 Input-agnostic, fully rebindable functional UX via GUIDE (drag-select on stick/touch too). Modern RTS/TBS command set: select by type/on-screen/control group, move in formation / to new formation, camera bookmarks, consolidated multi-squad ability menus, etc.
- R1Q5 Everything free except story campaigns + cosmetic non-P2W content (faction skins). Not profit-driven.
- R1Q6 Parallel play is THE differentiating pillar; solo/challenge runs become high-stimulation puzzles.
- R1Q7 Determinism non-negotiable.
- R1Q8 Simulated time-sliced WeGo execute. Vision: whole-campaign replay rendering overworld movement + all battles in real time in one unified view.
- R1Q9 Per-model sim; Squad = command unit; Unit overrides squad defaults.
- R1Q10 Player creates a self-insert Commander, but campaigns may cast the player as other commanders, or several. Tutorial "Angelfall": Jeff, conspiracy theorist on a backwater planet, last person outside the hangars, evacuating civilians for the planetary AI locked out of hangar controls. Jeff recurs. Characters/plots not yet written: this interview should shape them.
- R1Q11 Parent factions playable, splinters = doctrine layer; Ruin/Legivore/Tree non-playable threats; Angels/Voidfarers neutral/event.
- R1Q12 Time-linear SC/BW-style story starting with a human character. Ambition (stretch): story state machine flexible enough for faction-order-independent play.
- R1Q13 Story = EoAT; tutorial in a "first contact" sub-era just before EoAT. Earlier eras exposition only.
- R1Q14 Fraying Law makes local reality flexible; re-enacting legends makes legendary results possible and self-perpetuating. Explains militia vs warmachines. Existential war; humanity survives by overcoming impossible odds repeatedly.
- R1Q15 Magic: Raw = primal narrative force, can do anything, needs massive ritual framework to direct. First = Law, OS with registry + audits. Second = illicit/fragmented access to Law endpoints via narrative backpressure, ritual shims, exploits (NOT degraded capability). Ruin = scouring: raises costs, lowers efficacy, restricts types; Null = no magic. Ruin effects as (effect descriptor, target descriptor, magnitude). Plots and battlefield areas vary; Ruin ~ DoS/500 errors regardless of access path.
- R1Q16 Yes: after shared understanding, repair corruption, move stale docs to History/, rewrite consolidated GDD + decision log.

## Settled (Round 2)
- R2Q1 Armies grow over a campaign (HoMM/SC); story may carry armies across campaigns. Battleforce caps from campaign/battle rules. Commander picks a battleforce, then either drops it off (can leave at start of any turn if commander squad not deployed) or stays for commander bonuses + reinforcements from remaining army; leaving frees overworld movement. Battles begin at start of a turn (sync). Battleforce auto-fill: default strongest-until-cap, archetype presets sorting on squad features, player presets persist. Typical battle ~8 turns (T1 scout/position, T2 first casualties, T3-5 main engagement + reinforcements, T6 victor emerges, T7+ rout/scoring) ≈ 10 min at full timers. Hard cap empirical (perf testing across systems/graphics levels), not early concern. Story mission ~40 min (varies). Skirmish ~2 h (30 min–8 h+ configurable). Ranked like HotA Jebus Cross/Duel; expected default ranked = single battle, pre-built armies; full ranked match 30–40 min ending in a decisive battle (deception vs timing). Turn/timing system must be flexible + isolated so overworld/battles can run separately or together for experimentation.
- R2Q2 Timers in all modes; difficulty scales; untimed accessibility option flagged; challenge/ranked locked.
- R2Q3 Shared pulse (B) default; independent clocks (A) as configurable option.
- R2Q4 KEEP reaction window (paused sim after reveal; players scan all battles and issue reactions); configurable off. Conditional-trigger orders rejected as too deep; contextual auto-reactions OK as upgrades/unit abilities.
- R2Q5 Continuous fixed-point space. R2Q6 No hidden RNG; accumulators; seeds only for content gen.
- R2Q7 Legend = yes; revisit in depth (narrative + game).
- R2Q8 Law is built on raw magic and governs access; where Law fails, magic fails — raw does NOT become available (except wacky circumstances).
- R2Q9 Timeline error. Humans long-lived (functionally immortal on established planets). First contact → Angelfall ≤1000 yrs; EoAT shortly after Angelfall (5–30 yrs fits). Separate timeline grilling session to come.
- R2Q10 First contact = Lawbringer diplomats + Law extended over human space. Angelfall = humanity's broken history + Law-divination-circumvention tools (proto-Legivore) + Law's narrative pressure → lensing showing counterfactuals = "angels" (things people wish they'd done/said; "messengers from nonsense"). Unaddressed, angels build a counternarrative (sand barrier vs water). Angelfall = planetary failure → evacuation; spreads from where Law weakest; eventually whole systems. Tutorial = early Angelfall on frontier planet; Jeff spotted the pattern via schizoid paranoia; shows absurd options can be right, but he's wrong about everything else; mishandled → retreats to seclusion, stops helping.
- R2Q11 yes. R2Q12 experiment, solid start.
- R2Q13 Yes, but story-essential commanders revive only on easy; otherwise their death fails the mission (HoMM/SC).
- R2Q14 yes. R2Q15 minimal now, architected to be extensible toward deep resource modeling (audience loves it).
- R2Q16 Decide modding architecture EARLY. Study Factorio (Lua, perf) and SC2 Galaxy. Start with data/asset mods; dedicated question wave on modding. [research agent running]
- R2Q17 yes. R2Q18 yes + info-hiding abilities (disguise, hallucinate, cloak).

Camera context rules: follow commander → tab into its battle → swap battles → exit returns to followed commander; selecting another commander in a battle exits to that commander; deselect-all (allowed anywhere) exits to the current battle's overworld location.

## Settled (Round 3)
- R3Q1 ~60 s default plan budget; keep reserve top-up shape; cadence is general case, not max reserve. Need mitigation for players in many battles. Reaction window is a "treat" for players in battles: does NOT draw reserve; can be asymmetric (more battles → more reaction time). Encourage engaging with parallelism.
- R3Q2 Playback NOT counted against timers. Watching real-time execution locks out overworld view/interaction; ranked likely skips to results after reactions. After resolve: a brief per-turn change summary step.
- R3Q3 Commanders have abilities (not all battlefield / not every category). Commanders anchor armies; the ARMY's presence enables reinforcements. Future maybe: army drops battleforce without commander (limited). Reactions tied to commander: start with it.
- R3Q4 Reactions = restricted orders triggered by interactions arising from revealed orders (e.g., A and B come into range). Involved squads get a reaction opportunity if player is attentive. UI: radial popup w/ clear icons on reactable squads; scrollable reaction queue. Cost to squad and/or commander. One reaction timing, no counter-reactions except niche abilities. Stats wave needed (tabletop abstractions remain).
- R3Q5 Not one Pressure gauge: multiple systems — Morale, Endurance, Reflex, Mythos. Goal: don't overwhelm; designers tune squads/engagements to "feel right" without prescriptive optimization.
- R3Q6 SC2/WC3-style drop-in fine; "FPDR" + simple AoE now; later explore Dawn of War weapon-type × armour-type interactions.
- R3Q7 Accuracy = unit × weapon × activity × enemy state/abilities. Examples: moving full-auto > hits but worse accuracy than stationary aimed burst; ambush point-blank on unaware target best; robotic vector calculators ignore movement; Lawbringer in Ruined zone unfamiliar with fallback sidearm; intangible ignores mundane bullets. No generic +1 upgrades; gear per-unit (5 plasma rifles from cache → 5 units fire them). Big units: Mythos/performance-based or expensive overworld plot upgrades. Endurance = pressure vs high-exertion (sprint, sustained running, climbing, building, digging). Reactions under Reflex. Baseline Competence removed. Scavenging free for now (survivors pick up squad upgrades); wargear logistics later as setting/mode.
- R3Q8 yes (terrain/LOS/cover).
- R3Q9 Separate discovery session (different format) on effect language; study Path of Exile; look for pitfalls.
- R3Q10 yes; context-aware formula tooltips. Example: Bad Hit = hit > 50% of unit HP → shows (5HP) on 10HP unit, (Any Hit) on 1HP; +25% BASE HP buff counted (6.25HP), +25% TEMP HP not; "Sturdy" doubling threshold shows 10HP in green (beneficially modified).
- R3Q11 No dedicated servers (no cheap option for a free game). Draw on BAR & HoMM. Separate server/client from the start, even SP: client only receives/simulates what its server tells it. Every player runs a server deferring to an authoritative host (host can change during game); local sim progresses while awaiting host state; client gets partial updates from local server immediately, eventual consistency with host. Cheating fog requires being/impersonating host → flag vectors (seed-determined host timesharing, conflicting host claims). Plus replay auditing (data-value mismatches vs versioned data), BAR-like replay hosting site + community reports. Remove cheaters rather than prevent every attack.
- R3Q12 yes (Duel first, Campaign Duel, Glicko-2, pinned rulesets). R3Q13 yes (seeded template map gen). R3Q14 yes (utility AI).
- R3Q15 Simple War does NOT conclude the story. Humanity = latecomer + catalyst for the Legivore, destroying magic at its root, rippling out, calling back predators long gone from this corner of reality. Halo-like scope. Core theme: how do individuals/organizations reconcile an all-out fight for existence with staying human, when defeat could leave nothing to know what human was. Story outline needs updating.
- R3Q16 Lawmakers = old stub. Lawbringers: primarily Gardener, any species can join (magically ascertained/reinforced dedication; First Magic compensates for physical/intellectual limits). First human Lawbringers = future story beat. Org sustaining the Law's rituals, divisions: diplomacy, peacekeeping, humanitarian aid, warfare. Game: separate faction from Gardeners, NO subfactions; each Lawbringer identical, or a named character with emergency powers.
- R3Q17 Angelfall builds over long time; locally unstoppable once triggered; enough Lawbringers backed by a node and unsabotaged could stop one. Player prevents an Angelfall ~once in the story (dramatic). Angels = filler/interference: battles in front of resources, nonsense dialogue eating turn timers, stealing/moving overworld items/resources. Every angel not fought becomes one to face later → the preventable-Angelfall campaign (consequences win or lose). Jeff easter egg (if relationship maintained, shortly before that campaign): "angels almost never show up to a battle they can't lose". Mechanic: auto-resolve/surrender normally favours big armies (enemies may disband); angels invert it — a commander with NO battleforce can temporarily disperse angels by engaging; needed to clear movement-blocking angels in the Angelfall mission; doesn't work for the final key battles. Angelfalls rare. Counterfactual-mirror proposal "largely correct".
- R3Q18 yes (disposition in sim; Dialogic presentation only).
- R3Q19 Legivore named for what people see (devouring Law) but actually KILLS MAGIC (acetone on styrofoam). Can etch restrictions out of the Law locally; simplest use: render down & burn. Ruined areas = Law scoured beyond function; magic scoured entirely = null zone. Narrative refs need checking.
- R3Q20 yes (context stack).
- R3Q21 Star chart retired; overworld more granular. Battlefields: start 2D isometric, desaturated terrain for squad/unit contrast, simple assets; better presentation later.
- R3Q22 Hex-grid overworld; each hex = a plot (keep nomenclature). Need chase/intercept handler for commander interactions.

## Settled (Round 4)
- R4Q1 Intermission: own step, doesn't touch reserve. Cap default 30 s (non-ranked), 10 s ranked. If >=50% players ready → drop to 3 s (if not already lower). After intermission, summary collapses to a menu icon; all past turn summaries browsable there and during intermissions.
- R4Q2 Per-battle time bonus asymptotic: 2nd battle +10 s, each next slightly less, reaching +60 s total at 10 battles; beyond that +1 s per battle.
- R4Q3 Forecast continuously: as orders are planned (not locked), server forecasts interactions, keeps a reaction dict, revalidates through planning; final validation at ready/timer-out, then reactions offered. Same intent-ghost visuals as planning. UI refined in playtest.
- R4Q4 Pressure systems share one implementation; start at neutral, seek neutral homeostasis. Morale = governing system: floats systems that lose pressure, leeches from pressurized ones; NOT tied directly to damage/losses/being attacked (avoid perverse incentives). 5 unequal bands for all systems: Surging 90-100%, Primed 65-89%, Neutral 40-64%, Fraying 15-39%, Broken <15%. Broken morale leeches pressure from other systems → capability degrades per squad strengths/weaknesses (no arbitrary failed charges/ignored orders). Combat effects on morale shown as second-order. Morale generates N pressure units/turn; distributes proportionally (by capacity, toward equalizing %); systems don't equalize with each other — Morale is gate/governor.
- R4Q5 Endurance suffers direct damage + exertion ("vacuum"). Neutral = 100% speed/efficacy; vacuum proportionally reduces run bonus, dig/climb/build speed etc. relative to action cost; Endurance below neutral receives proportionally more of Morale's distribution.
- R4Q6 Reflex pressured by reaction + precision: affects detection range, damage point, moving-accuracy penalty, turn radius/speed, maybe crit chance/damage. Pressured by shooting on the move, being selected for Reaction, overwatch/detection abilities. Possibly split Reflex/Awareness vs Precision/Nimbleness.
- R4Q7 Legend (unit stat) to be designed. Holding pressure in battle → minor bonuses; only Surge or Break gives any persistent effect.
- R4Q8 FPDR = Fixed Point Defined Rounding (not a damage model).
- R4Q9 accuracy model yes.
- R4Q10 Army inventory ("War Chest"?): sort/filter list, icons + hover tooltips; click → detail with equip-to-valid-units. Equipped tab by squad for swapping. Manage anytime on overworld outside battle. Pickups show equip option. Auto-equip rules later. Limited upgrades re-equipped up to max next turn after casualties. Refitting = special overworld plots for vehicles/monsters/unique units. Legend surge upgrades/evolutions in intermission popup if immediate; if refit needed, a notice line.
- R4Q11 yes + need battle test harness + metrics to analyse results vs configuration changes.
- R4Q12 Hexes have FEATURES (from map-gen passes, player activity, natural events), not prescriptive terrain types. "Route" = commander's planned hex path; quick actions on route (drop off battleforce, pick up caches); Activities (refit, recruit, commander-led battle, convert resource node) require ending the route there.
- R4Q13 Yes + battleforces may be dropped off up to 1 hex from route (costs movement). Routing onto a battle tile ends all movement; must remove that segment to route elsewhere.
- R4Q14 yes (neutral guards).
- R4Q15 Zoom past threshold with a battle hex in context (selected/centered hex, selected commander on battle hex) → transition to that battle at default zoom. Battles menu for direct focus. PiP movable battle displays remain.
- R4Q16 Need not be gecs, but data-oriented stack driving composable archetypes preferred. "Testing framework" = its own question wave (QA, perf, balance, feel; bulk scenarios vs changes). User unsure where the perf research came from.
- R4Q17 Accepted; need not be lockstep — fighting-game predict/buffer/reconcile.
- R4Q18 Unclear on "three rounds"/"desync tooling"; research Slay the Spire (+Godot) modding & Workshop (haiku subagent running).
- R4Q19 Wants explanation of packages vs plain JSON ingestion before deciding.
- R4Q20 yes, but ranked allows ONLY official widgets/tools.
- R4Q21 Ruin = zone condition. Gardeners were late bloomers; galaxy/universe already picked over by early gods and their predators. World Tree = an early god (self-sustaining, self-limiting narrative). Stragglers called back by the rapidly unbinding Law (echoes of its impending birth+death), not the Legivore directly.
- R4Q22 Legivore BEFORE the story (main cause of Angelfalls; why the Law dies instead of reaching apotheosis). Separate narrative pass needed. Themes: First Empire never imagined it could get so bad → loses trust in itself; Law on brink of apotheosis. Tearers' horrors shook faith in divination (some interacted with divinations). Legivore undivinable (devoured divining magic) → divergence became the FIRST ANGEL. Law's pressure: careful dam for apotheosis → ocean of burning kerosene. Avoidance paths lay against the Law; Lawbringers followed impossible futures. All until Law's demise primed to become the LAST ANGEL (Law as it Could Have Been, perverted). Lawbringers denied humanity magic/tech (divined war, billions lost); showed humans their seeding, failed waystones, the embryonic veil; honesty culture; "guide but not provide" → humanity's entitlement erupted ("birthright"). Law vented pressure onto primitive human planets as harmless manifestations. Angels thought to have whispered the first Legivore to madmen; first deployment severed Sol's Law from the Empire; self-replicating probes; trillions of Legivore ramjets, most intercepted; countless worlds ruined. Empire triage: severed divination streams, ruined conveyance; zero-trust, manual rites, Gardener lives consumed; Law dragged backward at apotheosis; showed visions of ruin/betrayal → first Lawbringer splinter: TRUE BELIEVERS. Humanity saw none; angels vanished (Law couldn't vent) → pressure built unchecked; humanity rejoiced. Then first Angelfall = tutorial (Jeff).
- R4Q23 Human factions: HUMANITY FIRST (Legivore faction; fringe dissidents, shadowy benefactors, terrorist splinter cells; Legivore stockpile; powerful charismatic commanders, discounted infantry/gear, weak upgrades/heavy armour; independence/superiority propaganda vs Empire colonizers; officers dissatisfied with CHI in contact with True Believers/warmongers; gateway for other factions onto non-Law worlds; first into the fray; where player hardship choices matter most — defy leadership, beat odds). CHI = Conglomerate for Human Integration (UTG-backed cooperative w/ Lawbringer ties; most advanced military, Lawbringer-trained operatives, limited First Magic; humanitarian "good guys"; bonus objectives pull attention, high-maintenance characters & bureaucrats for every penny, denied the superweapon; traditional campaign). THE ENLIGHTENED (glimpsed the Law, think they can do better; want a brilliant strategist to play warlord, end war fast, unfreeze UTG-frozen assets, settle far from the Law to prove it). Message must go deeper than shallow themes.
- R4Q24 yes. R4Q25 Lawbringers/Angels not traditional factions. Lawbringer campaigns: raid-boss-tier individual commanders + few auxiliaries/constructs. PvP Lawbringers = commander power, BW-Protoss-like (rigid, force pace, punish, snowball). Angels = undirected narrative faction.

## Settled (Round 5)
- R5Q1 Pressure contract accepted: per-system pool with capacity; % maps to 5 bands; actions/events create vacuum or add pressure; each system drifts toward Neutral at its own rate; Morale is the only channel between systems (pays into below-Neutral systems weighted by capacity × deficit, leeches from above-Neutral); exchange runs on a fixed tick interval during Execute.
- R5Q2 ALL pressure systems are squad-level. When a surge benefit needs an individual (e.g. an Instinct surge's single crack shot, a Legend surge's special ability/upgrade), a "leader" unit is elected from the squad to use it.
- R5Q3 Morale generation: primarily a base rate. Not all commanders feed morale. Holding objectives is a source for some squads, not universal. Contagion and battle-event inputs dropped for now (deeper systems later). Parameterise every value in morale/pressure maths to find which tropes emerge (stability pull growing with distance? surge resets after a turn vs escalating cost? bespoke band ranges, neutral value, start point per squad?). All of it data-driven and easy to audit and edit.
- R5Q4 No direct band multipliers in the system. Effects are a function of composition: if high Instinct raises a squad's accuracy, that squad's data says so. Sensitivity profiles and band traits are content paths to build out.
- R5Q5 Yes: one stat for now (split Precision later only if needed); crits as deterministic "every Nth hit" accumulators.
- R5Q6 Name: Legend. Squad-level, like all pressure systems; sensitivity profiles tune what touches Legend and what Legend touches. Surge/Break give persistent changes, but not "Scars" (too 40K): consequence categories that feed the squad's story, not strictly bonus/malus. Example: an honour guard's broken Legend lowers its fire priority for enemy autobattle, hurting its story but enabling a break-away gambit through overwhelming fire. For now Legend only floats/sinks from archetypal actions (damage dealers dealing damage, tanks tanking, scouts revealing/ambushing, sappers hitting buildings/vehicles). Need easy nomenclature for rising/falling (Float/Sink?). Consequence pools match the squad archetype, but beware boring results ("the killer kills faster"); if Legend moves through Morale, the pool should be influenced by what is boosting Morale. Leave room for analytics to plug into pressure systems. Story: track Legend surges/breaks across the campaign; in the Angelfall mission, add or replace angel forces with squads built from the options NOT chosen (offer 3, pick 1; the other 2 combine into the angel version).
- R5Q7 Health layers yes, except armour: replace "flat reduction from gear" with a DEFENSIVE PROFILE grouping layers: flat DR, % DR, evasion, healing and absorption, resistances, other typical stats.
- R5Q8 Per unit: HP, Defensive Profile, Size Class, Movement Profile (movement types and speeds, acceleration, deceleration, turning rate), Offensive Profile (base proficiencies, equipped weapon statistics), Traits, Equipment. Per squad: Morale, Endurance, Reflex, Prowess, Legend (each with starting value, neutral value, drift rate, sensitivity profile), Total Hit Points, Formation Profile (formation, coherence, stance/mode), Keywords (status effects likely here too), Value, Capture Rate (for contesting objectives). Retire the old Statline, weapon_skill, Baseline Competence. RENAME pressure systems to SIMPL: Stamina (was Endurance), Instinct (was Reflex), Morale, Prowess (new), Legend.
- R5Q9 The drop-off rule covers every case where the commander isn't needed to oversee but forces are: neutral garrisons/guards that cause battles make the player either commit the commander or drop off a battleforce. Do NOT reveal what overworld forces will do before engagement (if a route plans through, the player learns nothing extra). Instead, bank a portion of movement points for commanders whose plan was cut short by a battle or a garrison negotiation. Maybe experiment later with conditional "move on after winning" planning; hesitant without testing.
- R5Q10 Not HoMM towns as hex features; closer to Civilization-style production without tending cities. Hex features provide production and recruitment activities where the player negotiates logistics (like visiting HoMM dwellings/mines). Some let the player select, upgrade or change what is produced; most production can set up routes for items and squads to rally to a location or commander. Distributed logistics encourages multiple commanders, consolidating battleforces, protecting supply lines. Future: faction/allegiance variants (e.g. a Humanity First feature hostile to other species, a government armoury with support tiers). For now: simple squad / item / resource tiles. Revisit consolidated build trees if this doesn't work.
- R5Q11 Start simple: VALUE is the primary resource: recruit commanders and squads, buy equipment. Story may experiment with: collectible assets traded at merchants, trading controlled-feature production for Value at a ratio, and investments (meta-campaign currency aligned with faction resources, giving a Value stipend or bonuses). The real scarce resource is multitasking; battles are already limited by battleforce Value. Supplies/Materiel/Manpower collapse into Value for now; trial other economies once the core loop settles or needs spice.
- R5Q12 Yes: one Humanity faction, three splinters (Humanity First, CHI, Enlightened) on a shared core roster.
- R5Q13 Spine is fine for eventually. Start with ONLY Angelfall: build the campaign tools, using Angelfall to learn what's convenient or inscrutable.
- R5Q14 Yes, foreshadowing only. World Tree = a god that stayed: crippled transcendence, self-sustaining, local apotheosis, can be materially eradicated; passed over like a sea anemone/urchin, not worth picking apart. Returning predators = bottom-feeders: transcendent beings who accept material existence for fewer threats and more abundant low-quality sustenance. World Tree followers may be a future faction. Only the Law, the Law As It Could Have Been, or the Legivore can divert the predators.
- R5Q15 Yes: True Believers = campaign-only antagonist using Lawbringer assets.
- R5Q16 Human base faction fleshed out for Duel, with Humanity First / CHI / Enlightened as variant options. Lawbringers next.
- R5Q17 Needs more research first. GECS examples were the original prototype plan. Archetypes as templates for composition and communication ("infantry archetype with x variants"): a repository of them is useful. Systems = functions that mutate state; call order owned by the caller (asked: does "fixed" mean consistent and repeatable? — yes). Unclear on the benefit of separate overworld/battle worlds. GDScript for now; native only on hitting limits. [research running]

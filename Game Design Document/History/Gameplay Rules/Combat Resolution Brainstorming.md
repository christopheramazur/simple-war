We want to look at a number of combat resolution methods and simulation approaches.

The goal is that the method of combat resolution can slot into the gameplay as a modular piece, mapping underlying data differently depending on how testing pans out.

The default game mode we want to begin our approach with is "simulating combat engagement from units in squads over a short period of time as they execute their orders"

This is similar to giving orders like "attack move toward that location" to an RTS unit group. The squad will move and engage enemies within a certain range of its path and destination, prioritizing based on narrative interaction if they're not told otherwise

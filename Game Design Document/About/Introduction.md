Major Pivot from Boardgame-based to Digital First.

Simple War is intended to be a heavily simulationist approach to fictional war games when compared to existing alternatives. The Total War franchise occupies this space for historical and fantasy games, with Total Warhammer 40K bringing a big of scifi to the mix. Heroes of Science and Fiction also tries to break into this space. Simple War intends to bring a turn-based approach to largescale squad warfare, with simultaneous resolution for the execution of squad actions. The game bases its resolution mechanics around the capabilities of a squad of average human soldiers, with the goal of allowing for massive aliens and advanced technologies to feel truly devastating, while still being vulnerable to heroic pluckiness and the occasional inhaled hand grenade. 

Simple War is a modular system built to provide players meaningful strategic and tactical options at every stage of the game. Battleforces are managed at a strategic level involving simultaneous decisions made by both players. Tactical encounters are built around a momentum-heavy combat resolution system, allowing the individual strengths of the units involved to shine depending on how well they've been managed.

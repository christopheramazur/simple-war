# The Pure
## Summary Blurb
Extreme isolationists who eschew alterations to their minds and bodies. 

## General Overview
Splintered from the Gardeners hundreds of millions of years before the Seeding, the Pure know nothing about the beneath, and only know about other species and the various ongoing conflicts in the galaxy through their observatories. 

They still have incredible mastery of material sciences, but their lifetimes are more in-line with human standard. Heavy use of automation machinery ensures they are able to live fulfilling lives in pursuit of the moment. 

# Lawmakers
## Summary Blurb
Diviners. Spellcrafters. Practitioners. The chosen few, called to serve. 

## General Overview
Empowered with the raw magic they help keep leashed, the stations of the Law are the most impactful an individual can aspire to -- and the most isolating.

Despite the overwhelming majority of Lawmakers being Gardeners who have touched the divine before and will again in the future, the training is mandatory with every enlistment. The physical, mental, and magical requirements are exacting, and even a short millennia away is enough to have lost touch with . The routine and schedule leave little room for individuality.

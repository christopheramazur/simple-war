Gardeners are generally human in appearance but heavily augmented with sleek machinery and biological systems that primarily serve to enhance senses. 

# Unaugmented "Pure" Gardeners
- Large sensory organs, lots of them
    - body is hairier than a typical human's
        - hair is sensitive to vibration, airflow
        - hair enhances proprioception in many ways
    - head is ridged along the top
        - hair thins along ridge
        - ridge is temperature-sensitive. 
        - typically an erogenous zone
    - larger nose, longer nose
    - manipulating tongue, tasting tongue
        - integrates with sinuses to enhance coverage and fidelity
    - larger ears, articulate
    - scent and heat sensing membranes on arms, legs
    - padded fingers
        - tastebuds deeply embedded, pressing hard allows contact

- bias toward reaching, manipulating; lacking strength overall compared to other hominids
    - limbs are typically longer
        - average for Gardener is 95th percentile human
    - vascular system adapted to multiple smaller hearts
        - blood pressure is typically 60-80 range
        - no adrenal systems    
    - hands grip both ways, grip is generally weaker than human
        - feet grip like their hands

- large vocal range
    - additional vocal modulators 
    - separate airflows for separate lungs
    - dual tongues and alternate sinus shape incorporate whistling
